Hey PH 👋

I built Site Mechanic because auditing a website meant juggling five tools — Lighthouse for speed, something else for SEO, another for accessibility, securityheaders.com, and nothing good for typography. Then merging it all into something a client could actually read.

Site Mechanic runs all five checks in parallel and gives you one ranked list: most critical first, each with the context you need to fix it.

What's free: 10 scans a day, all five categories, no signup.
What's paid: PDF export, saved reports, multi-page crawls ($29/mo, or a $20 day pass if you just have one big audit week).

Two things I'm proud of that took embarrassingly long:
- The report language locks at scan start — 8 languages, zero mixed-language reports.
- It scans sites behind bot protection (stealth headless Chrome) that block normal audit tools.

I'll be here all day — tell me what it gets wrong about your site.
